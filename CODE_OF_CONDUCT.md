# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

In short: be kind, assume good faith, and remember there is a person on the other end of every issue and PR.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language
- Publishing others' private information
- Sustained disruption of discussion
- Sharing exploits or credentials of real systems in public channels

## Enforcement

Reports go to `conduct@vayu.invalid` (replace before launch). Maintainers may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban contributors who breach the code. Maintainers are themselves bound by it.
